Sketch my own drill

Choose an ice template, then draw with a mouse, finger, or Apple Pencil, or place X and O markers.

Template:
Tool:Ink:Line:
Rink markings stay fixed. Draw is freehand. Straight lines and arrows are created by dragging from start to finish. For a curved line or arrow, drag from start to finish, then drag the visible midpoint handle to shape the curve. The handle remains editable until you choose another tool or begin another drawing. X and O are stamps and use the selected ink colour. Select lets you click one item or drag a box around multiple items; drag a selection to move it, then use Copy or Delete. Clear Markings removes only your annotations. Saving uses the selected template as the drill layout image.
